THE INTERFACE

A living view of the planet.

3D Earth by Perez keeps the planet in the foreground while connecting orbital, environmental, geographic and statistical information to a common visual context.

WHAT IS HERE

More than a globe.

01

Globe

Day and night illumination, atmosphere, clouds and orbital context.

02

Location

Browser positioning or WGS84 coordinates selected on the map.

03

Weather

Current conditions, local time and a short forecast for the selected point.

04

Environment & Earth systems

Earthquakes, fires, climate, air-quality, freshwater, oceans, weather, volcanoes and Earth-observation metadata, subject to each source coverage and cadence.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built to be useful beyond the globe.

3D Earth by Perez is designed as a common visual starting point for people who need geographic and planetary context.

SECTORS

GIS & geospatial · education · research · aviation & space · disaster awareness · journalism · environmental planning · development · community information · software development.

PEOPLE

Students · researchers · developers · teachers · journalists · planners · analysts · satellite enthusiasts · community organisations · public-information teams.

BENEFIT

See where something is, what source reports it, how fresh it is, and how it relates to the rest of the planet before moving to the specialist authority.

A NOTE ON THE DATA

Every layer has a source and a limitation.

Weather, orbital, environmental and development datasets have different update rates, geographic coverage and uncertainty. The source register explains what each layer means and what should not be inferred from it.
