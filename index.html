THE INTERFACE

The globe stays in the foreground.

The dashboard is deliberately sparse. Orbit the Earth, select a place, bring up a map or inspect local weather when you need it. Everything else stays out of the way.

WHAT IS HERE

Four useful views.

01

Globe

Day and night illumination, atmosphere, clouds and orbital context.

02

Location

Browser positioning or WGS84 coordinates selected on the map.

03

Weather

Current conditions, local time and a short forecast for the selected point.

04

Map

OpenStreetMap context for moving between the globe and a conventional map.

A NOTE ON THE DATA

The globe is a visualization, not an authority.

Position, weather and external feeds come from different systems and carry different limits. The source notes explain what is used, where it comes from and what should not be inferred from it.
