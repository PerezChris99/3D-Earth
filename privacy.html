Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you use 3D Earth's globe, location, map, weather and analyst features.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the public 3D Earth web application and its server-side routes. It does not override the privacy policies of third-party services that receive requests made on your behalf.

2. Location information

If you grant browser location permission, the application can receive latitude, longitude and the accuracy estimate supplied by your device. These values are used to center the globe and map and to request location-aware services such as reverse geocoding and weather.

Location permission is controlled by your browser. You can decline it and enter coordinates manually instead.

3. Technical and security data

The server may process request metadata such as timestamps, request paths, IP addresses and security events. This information may be used for service operation, rate limiting, authentication, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Retention depends on the deployment's logging configuration.

4. Third-party services

Map tiles, geocoding, weather data and external geospatial feeds are supplied by third parties. A request may contain the coordinates or query parameters required to return the requested information. Their own policies and terms apply to those requests.

See Data & Sources for the current provider list.

5. Your choices

You may refuse browser location access, use manual coordinates, disable optional layers and stop using the application. Do not place confidential, personal or sensitive operational information into analyst notes unless the operator of the deployment has established an appropriate data-protection process.

ImportantThis document describes the current application behaviour. A deployment with additional analytics, authentication, logging or integrations may require a separate privacy notice.
