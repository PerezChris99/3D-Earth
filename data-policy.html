DATA, MAPS & ATTRIBUTION

Data & Sources

3D Earth combines multiple technical and public data sources. Their accuracy, licensing and availability are not identical.

OpenStreetMap

Interactive map tiles and geospatial data may be provided by OpenStreetMap contributors. Attribution is displayed on the map. OpenStreetMap's standard tile service is best-effort and has usage restrictions, so production deployments should use an appropriate provider or self-hosted infrastructure at scale.

Weather

Weather information is obtained through the configured weather gateway/provider. Forecast resolution and accuracy vary by region and model. Commercial deployments must use a provider/license appropriate to their use.

Location & geocoding

Browser geolocation supplies WGS84 coordinates and an accuracy estimate. Reverse geocoding may use a configured geocoding service and should be treated as contextual rather than authoritative address verification.

Satellite & intelligence feeds

Satellite, flight, earthquake and other feeds may come from third-party public services. Availability, latency and completeness vary. Always verify important information against authoritative sources.
