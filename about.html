About 3D Earth

3D Earth is a browser-based geospatial visualization project. It brings a navigable globe, location tools, map context and weather information into one interface.

Purpose

The project is intended for geographic exploration and situational context. It is designed around a simple principle: the Earth should remain the primary object on screen, while supporting information appears when it is relevant.

Interface

The globe is rendered with Three.js. Location can come from the browser's positioning service or from coordinates selected on the map. Weather is requested for the selected coordinates, and OpenStreetMap provides the conventional map view. Additional layers may include satellites, flights, seismic activity and other feeds where available.

Limitations

3D Earth does not create the underlying geographic, weather or satellite data. Each source has its own accuracy, update cycle, licensing terms and availability. A visual layer should therefore be treated as context rather than as a definitive operational record.

Device positioning can be affected by hardware, signal conditions and browser settings. Weather forecasts are model output. Live feeds can be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable.

Project scopeVisualization, geographic context and exploration. Not a navigation, emergency-response or safety-critical system.
